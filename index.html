What we actually do.

Contracts and capture

We win and hold the contracts. Where a vehicle is reserved for a certified small business, our delivery partner bids it instead.

Care delivery

Promotoras and nurse care managers, hired out of the counties they cover, billing chronic care management at $66.13 per patient per month and community health integration at $86.17.

Technology

myabuela calls on an ordinary telephone, in Spanish, every morning. No app, no smartphone, no broadband.

Milepost

Our accelerator. Outside companies run time bound pilots on our own field staff and enrolled panel, in New Mexico alongside HealthInno and in Oregon alongside Rainshadow Health Innovation. See the programme.
